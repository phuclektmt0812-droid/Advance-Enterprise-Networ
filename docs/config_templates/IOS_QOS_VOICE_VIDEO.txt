TVI CORE QoS — VOICE EF AND VIDEO AF31
========================================

STATUS
------
QoS policy is already generated by tools/build_lab.py and applied to:
- VLAN 80 subinterfaces: input marking policy TVI-MARK-VOICE
- WAN GigabitEthernet0/1: output policy TVI-WAN-OUT

The generated policy matches DSCP EF for voice, AF31 for video, reserves a
strict-priority 20 percent queue for EF, bandwidth 15 percent for AF31 and
uses fair-queue for default traffic. This template documents how to verify
and safely tune the existing policy; it is not a second policy to paste over
the generated configuration.

POLICY INTENT
-------------
1. Trust/mark voice only at the known voice VLAN edge.
2. Carry EF markings across the routed/WAN path.
3. Give EF a bounded priority queue to avoid starving other classes.
4. Give AF31 a guaranteed bandwidth share.
5. Leave remaining bandwidth to class-default.
6. Apply the WAN egress policy at the actual bottleneck interface.

VERIFY CONFIGURATION
--------------------
show policy-map
show policy-map interface GigabitEthernet0/1
show running-config interface GigabitEthernet0/1
show running-config | section TVI-WAN-OUT
show access-lists

TEST PLAN
---------
1. Confirm voice test host is connected to VLAN 80 and the interface input
   policy is attached.
2. Generate a known EF-marked UDP stream and confirm TVI-VOICE counters rise.
3. Generate AF31-marked traffic and confirm TVI-VIDEO counters rise.
4. Generate concurrent best-effort traffic so the WAN egress is congested.
5. Compare policy counters, drops and application behavior during congestion.
6. Repeat with one WAN path disabled and confirm policy remains attached to
   the active egress path.

IMPORTANT
---------
A policy counter increasing does not prove good voice quality. Actual
congestion, delay, jitter and loss measurement are outside this implementation
pass as requested. Crypto encapsulation may change where classification and
queuing need to occur; inspect the real egress interface and image support.
Do not raise the priority percentage without checking platform guidance.
